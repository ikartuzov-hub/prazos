Rules that always apply

Line on your recibo verified

While income stays under €15,000, write on the recibo: Sem retenção, nos termos do n.º 1 do artigo 101.º-B do Código do IRS. Without it, the company withholds 23%.

Source: CIRS art. 101.º-B

EU clients: recapitulative statement to confirm

After the 2025 changes the rule is ambiguous for the art. 53 regime. Confirm with an accountant or AT.

Source: RITI · DL 35/2025

Invoice everything verified

Fatura-recibo on the day of payment; fatura by the 5th working day; EU services by the 15th of the next month. Advances included.

Source: CIRS art. 115.º · CIVA art. 36.º

Work accident insurance verified

Mandatory while the activity is open.

Source: DL 159/99

Changes and closing verified

Changes: 15 days. Closing the activity: 30 days.

Source: CIVA art. 32.º, 33.º
